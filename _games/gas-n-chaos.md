### Welcome to the messiest Gas Station in VR! ⛽

Run your own station, serve a nonstop line of strange customers, and decide every shift: do the job properly, or turn the whole place upside down.\
**⛽ Fuel Cars & Serve Customers**Grab the nozzle, fill up tanks, take payment and keep the queue moving. Fast, clean service means bigger tips and happier drivers or you can "accidentally" spray fuel across the lot and watch it all fall apart. \
**🧼 Wash, Repair & Restore Vehicles**Scrub off dirt, swap damaged parts, fix engines and hand back cars in better shape than they arrived. Quality work builds your reputation and unlocks better-paying jobs. \
**🔧 Wreck, Grab & Go Wild**Or don't fix anything at all. Pull parts off cars, smash windows, prank the drivers and see how far you can push before someone notices. Every shift is a physics playground.  \
**🚚 Keep Your Tanks Full**Your station's fuel supply runs out as you serve. When the tank hits empty, call in the fuel truck and pay out of your own earnings to refill it. Manage your cash carefully no fuel means no customers, and no customers means no money.\
 **📦 Unlock New Gear From Deliveries**Spend your earnings on fresh tools, gadgets, station upgrades and unlockable equipment. Each new item opens up another way to work or another way to cause trouble. \
**🚔 Track Down Wanted Drivers**Keep an eye on the lineup. Some customers are on the sheriff's list spot them, stop them, and cash in the bounty. Sandbox chaos, silly physics, and a station that's yours to run… or ruin.
