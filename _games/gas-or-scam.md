Step into **Gas or Scam**, a physics-driven VR game where every shift at the pump is a choice between honest work and pure mayhem.

You're the owner of a rundown roadside gas station, and the line of customers never stops. Some just want a quick fill-up. Others need their car scrubbed, patched up, or rebuilt from a wreck. How you treat them — and their vehicles — is entirely up to you.

### 🚗 Serve the Line, Your Way
Handle the nozzle, top off tanks, collect payment, and keep traffic moving. Smooth, honest service earns trust, tips, and repeat customers. Or skip all that and let the fuel fly wherever it lands.

### 🛠️ Fix It Up or Tear It Apart
Wash away the grime, swap in new parts, and get engines running like new — solid repair work builds your reputation and opens the door to bigger jobs. Prefer chaos? Rip parts off, shatter glass, and turn every vehicle into scrap.

### 🎮 A Full Physics Playground
Nothing is scripted. Every prank, every mess, every "oops" moment plays out with real physics — because in this game, Gas or Chaos isn't just a name, it's a philosophy.

### ⛽ Manage Your Fuel Supply
Tanks run dry as you work. Call the fuel truck and pay for a refill out of your own pocket when supplies run low — spend wisely, because no fuel means no business.

### 🧰 Grow Your Station
Reinvest your earnings into new tools, upgrades, and unlockable equipment from deliveries. Every new gadget adds a fresh way to work — or wreck.

### 🚨 Catch the Wanted
Watch the queue closely — some drivers are on the sheriff's watchlist. Recognize them, stop them, and collect the bounty.

No two shifts feel the same. Run a tight, respectable operation or turn your lot into a demolition zone — in Gas or Scam, the mess is entirely up to you.
