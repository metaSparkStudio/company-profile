About Us

Small studio, hands-on craft.

MetaSpark Studio has been designing and building games since 2020, with a focus on two platforms we genuinely love working on: standalone VR headsets and mobile devices. We build experiences around real physics and player-driven systems rather than scripted moments — the kind of game where what happens next depends on what you decide to do with your hands.

Our team handles a project end to end: concept, prototyping, art, engineering, and the ongoing work of publishing and supporting a live title after launch. That full-cycle approach is what shipped our flagship VR title, Gas or Scam, to the Meta Horizon Store.

What We Do

Services

From first prototype to a live, updated title — here's where we help.

VR

VR Game Development

Native builds for Meta Quest and other standalone headsets, with hand-tracking, room-scale movement, and real physics interaction at the core.

MG

Mobile Game Development

iOS and Android titles built for quick sessions, smooth performance on a wide range of devices, and mechanics designed for long-term retention.

GD

Game Design & Prototyping

Concept development and rapid prototyping to test a core mechanic and find the fun before committing to full production.

LO

Publishing & Live Operations

Store listings, submission, post-launch updates, and community management across the Meta Horizon Store and mobile marketplaces.

3D

Art & Technical Production

Stylized low-poly environments and characters, built and optimized to hold a steady frame rate on standalone VR hardware.
